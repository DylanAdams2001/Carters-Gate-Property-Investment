Frequently Asked Questions

BRRR stands for Buy, Refurbish, Rent, Refinance, Repeat. You purchase a below-market-value property — typically with cash or short-term bridging finance — then add value through refurbishment (and sometimes an extension or loft conversion). Once tenanted, you refinance onto a buy-to-let mortgage based on the new, higher valuation, releasing the majority of your original capital to redeploy into the next deal. The strategy can also work on properties purchased with a mortgage, though capital recycling tends to be most effective on cash or bridged acquisitions. Done correctly, it’s one of the most powerful wealth-compounding strategies available to UK investors.
