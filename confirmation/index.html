Before the Call

Get the Most from Your 30 Minutes

💰

Know Your Borrowing Capacity

Have a rough idea of your borrowing capacity or speak to a broker beforehand. The more we know, the more tailored your roadmap will be.

🎯

Define Your Investment Goal

Think about what you want from property: monthly cash flow, long-term equity growth, or a mix of both — there’s no wrong answer.

❓

Jot down anything you want to cover — finance, compliance, timelines, returns, tax. No question is too basic or too advanced.
